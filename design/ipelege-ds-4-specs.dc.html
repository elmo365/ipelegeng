21 · ARRIVAL ATTESTATION
Both sides answer one question. Agreement resolves silently; only a conflict costs anyone attention
How a cancellation resolves
1
Before the slot, no fee. A customer who cancels before the agreed time owes nothing, and the provider's commission is never taken.
2
After the slot, both attest. Each side answers whether the provider had arrived. Matching answers apply the outcome with no review.
3
Conflicting answers go to adjudication. Ipelege reads both statements and any photo. The wallet shows a pending reversal with no amount until it is decided.
4
Rides are the exception. Dispatch has a GPS trace, so arrival is known and no attestation is asked for.
STILL OPEN
The fee amount is not set. A percentage of the job, a flat pula figure, and a fee waived on the provider's first occurrence are all defensible — this needs a business decision, not a design one. Screens are written so the number can drop in without changing the flow.
Light & dark
One control, every screen
The Light / Dark / System control on the Preferences screen above drives every phone in this document. Switch it and scroll back — the whole journey follows, because each screen reads its colours from one palette object rather than hardcoding them.
check_circle
Surfaces lift, they do not just darken. Dark cards sit lighter than the page behind them, keeping the same raised-card reading as light mode.
check_circle
Brand blue lightens. #145A8D fails contrast on a dark surface, so text and icons switch to a lighter tint while the gradient hero keeps the deep brand blue.
check_circle
Status colours desaturate the fill, brighten the text. A green verified chip on dark uses a muted green ground with a bright green label, never the light-mode pair inverted.
check_circle
Shadows go black, not blue. The blue-tinted light-mode shadow disappears on dark surfaces, so opacity carries the elevation instead.
FOR FLUTTER
Both palettes map to a single ThemeData pair. Category hues are computed from one hue value per category, so a tenth category needs one number, not a new colour set. System mode reads MediaQuery.platformBrightness.

Navigation, back & returning
Android has a system back gesture that users trust more than anything on screen. These rules say what it does on every screen in this document, and what the app remembers when it is killed and reopened.
What system back does
arrow_back
Tab roots return to Home, then exit. Back from Bookings, Wallet or Account lands on Home. Back from Home exits the app — it does not cycle through previously visited tabs.
arrow_back
Sheets and dialogs close, they do not navigate. One back gesture dismisses the top layer only. A sheet over a screen takes two gestures to leave the screen.
arrow_back
Multi-step flows step back one field group. Verification, create listing and booking hold their entered values while stepping backwards — nothing is cleared until the flow is abandoned deliberately.
report
Back out of unsaved work asks first. Only where real typing or an upload would be lost: create listing, verification, a written review. Never on a screen the person only read.
block
Back is blocked in two places. During an OTP verification round trip, and while a payment or top-up is in flight. Both show progress instead, because the result is already server-side.
swap_horiz
Mode switching resets the stack. Entering provider mode starts a fresh stack at provider home; back from there returns to customer home, not into the provider history.
What survives being killed
Cheap phones kill backgrounded apps often. Reopening should not feel like starting over.
check_circle
Half-finished verification uploads, stored locally until submitted.
check_circle
A listing draft, including photos already picked.
check_circle
The tab you were on, and the scroll position in a long list.
check_circle
Provider online state, and the ride you were on.
cancel
Not kept: search text, filter selections, and any OTP already sent.
FOR FLUTTER
Tab roots use nested Navigators with a PopScope at each root. Drafts persist to local storage on every field change, not on a save action.
